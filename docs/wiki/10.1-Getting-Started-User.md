## Overview

This guide helps new users get started with Tickflow. It covers account creation, email verification, the onboarding flow, and basic navigation. By the end of this guide, you will have a fully configured account ready to start analyzing stocks.

---

## Creating an Account

### Step 1: Navigate to Sign Up

1. Open your browser and go to: [https://sentiment-driven-stock-price-predic.vercel.app/](https://sentiment-driven-stock-price-predic.vercel.app/)
2. Click the **"Sign Up"** button in the top right corner

### Step 2: Complete the Registration Form

Fill in the registration form with the following information:

| Field | Description | Requirements |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Username** | Your unique username | 3-30 characters, letters, numbers, underscores |
| **Email** | Your email address | Valid email format |
| **Password** | Your password | 8+ characters, not too common |
| **Confirm Password** | Re-enter your password | Must match the password above |

### Step 3: Submit the Form

1. Click **"Create Account"**
2. You will be redirected to the email verification page

---

## Email Verification

### Step 1: Check Your Email

1. Open the email from **Tickflow Capital** (check spam if not found)
2. The email will contain:
   - A **"Verify Email"** button
   - A **6-digit verification code**

### Step 2: Verify Your Email

**Option A: Using the Link**

1. Click the **"Verify Email"** button in the email
2. You will be redirected to the verification page
3. Your email will be automatically verified

**Option B: Using the Code**

1. Go to the verification page: `https://sentiment-driven-stock-price-predic.vercel.app/verify-email`
2. Enter the 6-digit code from the email
3. Click **"Verify"**

### Resend Verification Email

If you didn't receive the verification email:

1. Go to `https://sentiment-driven-stock-price-predic.vercel.app/resend-verification`
2. Enter your email address
3. Click **"Resend"**

---

## Onboarding Flow

After email verification, you will be redirected to the onboarding flow. This 4-step process configures your account.

### Step 1: Profile Setup

| Field | Description | Required |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Nickname** | Your preferred display name | Yes |
| **Full Name** | Your full name | No |
| **Bio** | A short description about yourself | No |

1. Enter your nickname (this will be displayed on your profile)
2. Optional: Enter your full name and bio
3. Click **"Continue"**

### Step 2: Preferences

| Field | Options | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Investment Goal** | Growth, Income, Value, Trading, Retirement | Your primary investment objective |
| **Risk Tolerance** | Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive | Your comfort with risk |
| **Experience Level** | Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced | Your trading experience |
| **Persona** | Trader, Quant Researcher, Developer, Analyst, Student | Your role or interest |

1. Select your preferences
2. Click **"Continue"**

### Step 3: Watchlist

1. Enter a stock symbol in the search box (e.g., `AAPL`)
2. Click **"Add"** to add it to your watchlist
3. Repeat to add more stocks
4. Click **"Continue"**

### Step 4: Completion

1. Review your choices
2. Click **"Go to Dashboard"**
3. You are now onboarded and redirected to the dashboard

## Common Tasks

### Analyzing a Stock

1. Navigate to the **Dashboard**
2. Enter a stock symbol in the search bar (e.g., `AAPL`)
3. Click **"Analyze"**
4. View the analysis results:

| Section | What You See |
|---------|--------------|
| **Sentiment** | Overall sentiment (Bullish/Bearish/Neutral) with score |
| **Technical Indicators** | SMA, RSI, Support, Resistance, Volume |
| **Prediction** | LSTM prediction (UP/DOWN) with confidence |
| **Price Targets** | Bearish, Base, and Bullish price targets |

### Viewing Predictions History

1. Navigate to **"Predictions"** in the sidebar
2. View your prediction history table
3. Use filters to refine results:

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Symbol** | Filter by stock symbol |
| **Date Range** | Filter by prediction date |
| **Outcome** | Filter by Correct, Incorrect, or Pending |
| **Source** | Filter by LSTM or Sentiment |

### Managing Your Watchlist

1. Navigate to **"Dashboard"**
2. Go to the Watchlist section
3. To **add** a symbol: Enter symbol and click **"Add"**
4. To **remove** a symbol: Click the **"X"** icon on the symbol

### Updating Your Profile

1. Navigate to **"Profile"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Edit Profile"**
3. Update your information:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Nickname** | Your display name |
| **Full Name** | Your full name |
| **Bio** | A short bio |
| **Persona** | Your role or interest |
| **Preferences** | Investment goal, risk tolerance, experience |

4. Click **"Save"**

### Changing Your Password

1. Navigate to **"Profile"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Change Password"**
3. Enter:
   - **Current Password**
   - **New Password**
   - **Confirm New Password**
4. Click **"Save"**

### Generating an API Key

1. Navigate to **"Profile"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"API Key"**
3. Enter a name for the key (e.g., "Production")
4. Click **"Generate"**
5. **Save the key immediately** – it will only be shown once

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: I Didn't Receive the Verification Email

1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Use the resend verification option
3. Contact support: anyega.alex.kamau@gmail.com

### Issue: My Account is Not Onboarded

1. Go to `https://sentiment-driven-stock-price-predic.vercel.app/onboarding`
2. Complete the onboarding flow
3. If the issue persists, contact support

### Issue: I Forgot My Password

1. Go to `https://sentiment-driven-stock-price-predic.vercel.app/forgot-password`
2. Enter your email address
3. Follow the password reset instructions in the email

### Issue: I Can't Access a Feature

1. Check your account tier (Free/Pro/Enterprise)
2. Upgrade your account if needed
3. Contact support for assistance
