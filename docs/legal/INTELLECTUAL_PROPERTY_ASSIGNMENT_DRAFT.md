## Background

A. The Assignor has created or contributed to certain software and related materials for the project currently known as Tickflow Capital Intelligence, maintained at https://github.com/AnyegaAlex/Tickflow-Capital-Intelligence (the Project).

B. The Company intends to own, maintain, develop, license and operate the Project as a company asset.

C. The parties wish to document the transfer of the Assignor's transferable economic copyright rights, subject to third-party rights and the Copyright Act, Cap. 130.

## 1. Assigned Works

The works covered by this draft are the original materials created by the Assignor for the Project and listed or described in Schedule 1, including, to the extent owned by the Assignor:

- source and object code;
- backend and frontend application code;
- tests, scripts, build and deployment configuration;
- database schemas, original documentation and technical specifications;
- original UI layouts, graphics and written product content;
- original architecture, diagrams and project-specific materials;
- modifications and derivative works created by the Assignor for the Project.

The assignment excludes third-party and open-source materials, provider SDKs, datasets, fonts, icons, stock assets, generated materials whose rights are not owned by the Assignor, and contributions by any other person unless separately listed and validly assigned.

## 2. Assignment of economic rights

Subject to completion of the statutory recordal process, the Assignor assigns to the Company all transferable economic copyright rights owned by the Assignor in the Assigned Works, worldwide and for the full period of protection, including the rights to reproduce, publish, distribute, communicate to the public, adapt, translate, modify, make derivative works from, commercialise, license, sublicense and enforce those rights.

The assignment covers existing Assigned Works and future Project-specific works created by the Assignor to the extent permitted by section 33(5) of the Copyright Act.

## 3. Consideration

The assignment is made for valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which the parties acknowledge. The exact consideration and any tax/accounting treatment must be confirmed by the Company and its Kenyan adviser before execution.

## 4. Ownership warranties and disclosure

The Assignor represents, to the best of the Assignor's knowledge and subject to the exclusions above, that:

- the Assignor has authority to assign the economic rights stated in this document;
- the Assigned Works are identified accurately in Schedule 1;
- any third-party contributions, commissioned works, employment-created works and material licensed from others are disclosed in Schedule 2;
- the Assignor has not knowingly granted an inconsistent exclusive licence or assignment over the Assigned Works.

The Assignor does not purport to assign rights owned by another contributor, employer, commissioning party or third-party licensor.

## 5. Existing public licences

The parties acknowledge that the Project currently contains an MIT licence. This assignment does not revoke, narrow or prejudice any valid licence already granted to the public before the effective transfer. The Company will honour those existing licences.

After the statutory transfer is perfected, the Company may maintain or license future versions of the Project, subject to existing public licences and third-party licence obligations.

## 6. Moral rights

The parties acknowledge section 32 of the Copyright Act. The author's moral rights, including the right to claim authorship and the right to object to certain prejudicial treatment of the work, are distinct from economic rights and are not transferable during the author's lifetime.

Nothing in this document purports to assign those non-transferable moral rights. The Assignor consents, to the extent permitted by law, to ordinary maintenance, correction, adaptation, integration, translation and modification of the Project by or for the Company, while preserving the statutory position on moral rights.

## 7. Statutory recordal

The parties shall cooperate to lodge this written assignment with the Kenya Copyright Board and obtain the certificate of recordal and entry in the Assignment Register required by section 33(3A) of the Copyright Act.

The parties shall not represent the assignment as perfected or validly transferred until the statutory recordal requirements have been satisfied. The Company shall retain the executed instrument and certificate of recordal with its corporate records.

## 8. Further assurances

Each party shall sign reasonable further documents and provide reasonable assistance necessary to give effect to this assignment and its recordal, including correction of the Project's copyright notice after recordal.

## 9. Governing law

This document is governed by the laws of Kenya. The parties submit to the jurisdiction of the competent courts of Kenya, subject to any mandatory statutory procedure.
