## Background

The Company proposes to accept an assignment of transferable economic copyright rights in the Tickflow Capital Intelligence project from Anyega Alex Kamau, subject to confirmation of title, third-party rights, execution of the assignment and statutory recordal by the Kenya Copyright Board.

## Proposed resolutions

The directors, having considered the disclosed interest and the Company's constitutional documents, resolve that:

1. The Company may enter into the proposed economic copyright assignment for the project identified in the assignment instrument and its schedules, subject to Kenyan legal review.
2. [Name/position] is authorised to negotiate and execute the assignment on behalf of the Company, provided the authorised signatory is not disqualified by an undisclosed conflict.
3. The Company may incur reasonable legal and statutory filing costs and take the steps required to lodge the assignment with the Kenya Copyright Board under section 33(3A) of the Copyright Act.
4. The Company shall not update the repository's MIT copyright notice or represent itself as the perfected owner until the statutory recordal evidence has been received and retained.
5. After recordal, the Company may arrange transfer of the GitHub repository and related project assets to a company-controlled organisation and update ownership, billing, recovery and administrator controls.
6. The Company shall maintain the executed assignment, schedules, KECOBO certificate of recordal and repository-transfer evidence in its corporate records.
