##  5. Key Relationships & Insights

### Solar Potential + Structural Integrity + Financial Metrics
**Inputs:** Roof area, irradiance, shading, structural load capacity, electricity tariff
**Output:** Optimal system size (kWp), annual generation (kWh), payback period, ROI

### Thermal Performance + Energy Waste + Financial Impact
**Inputs:** Thermal images, wall materials, window specs, HVAC runtime
**Output:** Energy waste (kW), annual cost (KES), remediation payback

### As-Built Scan + Original Design = Deviation Analysis
**Inputs:** Point cloud, original drawings, material specs
**Output:** Deviation report, structural risk assessment

### Entry Points + CCTV + Lighting + Crime = Breach Probability
**Inputs:** Vulnerability scores, coverage %, crime data
**Output:** Security risk score, remediation plan, insurance impact

### Defects + Age + Climate = Deterioration Rate
**Inputs:** Defect register, building age, rainfall/sun exposure
**Output:** Lifespan forecast, maintenance schedule
